# First Light: arrival, fishing and discoveries

The player should arrive in a strange, welcoming food ecosystem. A title scene leads to five independent journeys, an original character, then a brief skippable in-world montage. Loading bypasses the montage. Old saves remain accessible and are never overwritten by New.

Fishing is a complete sequence: shoulder and rod cast, flying float, ripples and bite, moving fish inside a controllable catch zone, then retrieval and a flying ingredient. Hold mouse or Space to move the zone right, release to drift left. Good tracking earns progress; misses slowly drain it. Species and movement vary by island. Fishing is available along every shoreline and spring; visible schools identify productive habitats.

Exploration adds five island-specific responsive landmarks with journal entries and one-time ingredient rewards. These give routes a destination and connect scenery to the food loop. Ambient schools, drifting lights, animals and small reactions support the existing harvest ecology. This pass is a playable expansion, not the final large open-world campaign.

Character choices affect the real directional walking and action sprites, with a common native pixel grid and persistent palette/hair/face choices. UI uses the existing licensed Pixelify font and warm pixel frames. Options retain separate music/SFX, zoom, motion and display settings. The intro shows places, a friendly creature and a busy supper rather than control instructions.

References studied (ideas only; no copied imagery):
- [Environmental storytelling and choreography](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/env-art/storytelling): discoveries carry a small story and a visible action changes the scene.
- [Eastward's inhabited villages and food markets](https://chucklefish.org/blog/eastward-winter-development-update/): landscape, food and daily work belong together.
- [Stardew fishing](https://wiki.stardewvalley.net/Fishing): moving target, player-controlled catch zone, recoverable progress loss.

Execution guide: keep saves additive; use separate slot paths; block world input behind the front end; preserve all five island identities and protected road footprints; award only after animations; cancel safely; provide keyboard and mouse controls; verify actual title/new/load/appearance/intro in the Windows player, then replay the existing restaurant/ecology journey. Do not describe automated captures as human playtesting.

The finished content adds an original flowering-sail Pearlfin in Pearltide and Pearlfin Chowder: 20 ingredients and 21 dishes. Outer areas are 44×38, 40×58, 76×30, 44×68 and 78×42 world units, with their original docks/arrivals preserved.
