# First Light playtest

Extract the Windows ZIP and run `Wildfeast.exe`. Keep its data folder beside it.

## Arrival and your journeys

1. The game opens on the animated Wildfeast title. Try New, Load, Options and Exit.
2. Choose New. Enter a name and change skin, hair style/color, face, shirt, pants and boots. The walking preview should update immediately; Random also works.
3. Begin the journey. Watch the 24-second island-and-supper introduction, or use Skip introduction / Esc. This introduces the world rather than controls. Afterward, walking and tools should work.
4. Your character should keep the chosen look in every direction and while using tools.
5. Esc → Options → Display, controls and journey → Title screen saves and returns to the menu. New now creates the next empty slot. Load lists each journey's name, day and island. Return to the first one and check its character, inventory and progress.
6. The original pre-slot save appears as Continue original save. Five slots are available; New never overwrites an occupied one. When full, the menu explains how to free a slot after backing it up. There is no in-game delete button in this pass.

## Fishing

1. Equip the rod with slot 2 or click its hotbar icon. Stand on dry land beside a spring or shore on any of the five islands.
2. Aim toward water and left click / Space. Watch the chef cast, the flying float and the bite.
3. Hold mouse / Space to move the green catch zone right; release to drift left. Keep the moving fish inside it. Watch the catch percentage increase; deliberately miss and confirm it slowly decreases rather than instantly failing.
4. Finish the bar. Watch retrieval and the ingredient fly into your hands. Exactly one portion should enter your bag.
5. Repeat on Mistwake, Emberfold, Moonfen and Pearltide. Their fish differ: Bubblecarp, Lavafin, Jellyray and Pearlfin respectively; Saltleaf is home to Leafgill. Escape cancels without awarding a fish. A full satchel rejects the cast clearly.

## Explore beyond the familiar routes

Each island has a larger outer region linked to its existing routes. M shows the enlarged chart and your position. Walk to its new landmark, leaving room for two food portions:

- Saltleaf's forgotten dinner bell: interact to ring it.
- Mistwake's rain keeper kettle: water its cutting.
- Emberfold's caramel quarry: use the pickaxe.
- Moonfen's moon harp: clear its reeds with the scythe.
- Pearltide's homeward compass: interact to wake it.

Each reacts, reveals a world story, gives an ingredient and learns a dish. The kettle consumes one water unit. Repeat visits let you read the story without duplicating rewards. The journal records these alongside ingredient notes. Trees, stones and the landmark bases have footprints; the paths remain walkable and untillable.

## Sound and comfort

Title Options contains separate music/effect sliders, zoom defaults, motion preference and display mode. In-game Options retains the fuller sound, camera, challenge and controls pages. Title defaults apply to new journeys; each existing journey retains its saved in-game settings. Reduced motion keeps ambient movement quiet while preserving useful action animations.

This remains a five-island playable prototype, with 20 ingredients, 21 recipes and the existing restaurant loop. The introduction is a game-rendered montage. Larger campaigns, more discovery chains, finer character art and broader ecology still need iteration. Judge fishing feel, fresh-player clarity and exploration density during human playtesting; automated checks cannot settle those.
