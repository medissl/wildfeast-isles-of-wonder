# Wonder redesign playtest

This build replaces the previous archipelago layouts and cast. It remains a playable prototype; finished art density, progression balance and a fresh player's understanding still need human judgement.

## What changed

- Five actual world sizes: Saltleaf 40×26, Mistwake 32×38, Emberfold 52×24, Moonfen 36×44, Pearltide 56×32 units. Tall orchard/wetland, broad quarry and crescent lagoon have different routes. Ferry docking, camera limits, maps and planting follow their definitions.
- Separate creature anatomy: stock-pot boar, custard sheep, spice pangolin, winged dumpling moth and spiral kelp snail. Legacy ingredient/save IDs remain compatible.
- Body targeting and collection reach, calm/lure grace periods, persistent Brothback stock and visible ingredient readiness cues. A full bag doesn't consume an uncollected reward.
- Native chef walking/action frames, five visitor outfits, animated staff walking, biome canopies and rooted edible plants. Six original music themes and sound/zoom options remain.
- Whole centred soil footprints avoid paving and banks. Measured sign lettering stays inside the boards. Map pins respect each chart's different dimensions.

## Try these first

1. Collect every creature. Saltleaf: approach the boar, dodge its telegraphed charge, then move close and E/right-click for stock. Mistwake: stand quietly beside the sheep for two seconds, step closer and collect cream. Emberfold: click the pangolin's body with the pickaxe three times, then E/right-click for spice. Moonfen: hold a gathered Lanternroot ingredient on the hotbar, approach the moth and collect pollen. Pearltide: water the snail once, then collect jelly. The floating ingredient marks readiness; sources recover next morning.
2. Try a full satchel. Empty your ingredients at home and return to the ready creature. A failed pickup must not consume its harvest. Sheep/moth conditions can be triggered again; pangolin/snail readiness stays saved.
3. Test road edges. Shovel directly on paving and beside diagonal junctions. No part of the soil square should cover paving. Clear soil farther from the road should still plant and water. Boardwalks remain untillable.
4. Sail and explore all five islands. Check distinct arrivals, landmarks, habitats and routes. M should show the correct island shape and your location. Explore the taller islands' northern reaches and Emberfold's wide shelves.
5. Save on Pearltide and relaunch. Return to its southeast landing on dry ground. Money, ingredients, seeds, crops, upgrades and recipes should remain.
6. Inspect people and signs. Check all chef directions/tool actions, guests arriving, Nori carrying dishes, HARBOR TABLE lettering and OPEN/CLOSED boards. Try zoom, a smaller window, music/SFX and reduced motion.
7. Complete a service. Bring new ingredients home, cook/serve, receive payment and rest. This loop retains 19 ingredients, 20 dishes and six crops.
